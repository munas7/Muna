# Muna - Tower Defense Game

A browser-based tower defense game built with HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript.

## How to Play

1. **Open the game**: Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. **Select a tower**: Click on one of the tower buttons (Basic, Fast, or Strong)
3. **Place towers**: Click on the green area (avoid the brown path) to place your selected tower
4. **Start the wave**: Click the "Start Wave" button to spawn enemies
5. **Defend**: Your towers will automatically shoot at enemies in range
6. **Survive**: Keep your health above 0 and progress through waves

## Tower Types

- **Basic Tower** ($100): Balanced damage and fire rate
- **Fast Tower** ($150): Quick firing but lower damage
- **Strong Tower** ($200): High damage but slower fire rate

## Game Mechanics

- Each enemy that reaches the end reduces your health by 10
- Defeating enemies earns you money
- Completing a wave grants a $100 bonus
- Each wave spawns more enemies with increased health
- Use your money strategically to place towers

## Running the Game

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No build process or dependencies required!
